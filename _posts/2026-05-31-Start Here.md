## Welcome

Welcome to **We Love Scripture**.

This is a Christian podcast and ministry dedicated to helping believers grow in their love for God’s Word—not just by reading it, but by understanding it, living it, and being transformed by it.

If you are here for the first time, we are glad you found this space.

---

## What is We Love Scripture?

We Love Scripture is a Bible-centered podcast and teaching ministry focused on helping believers:

- Grow in their understanding of Scripture  
- Develop a consistent love for God’s Word  
- Strengthen their walk with Christ through biblical teaching  
- Engage deeply with Scripture in everyday life  

We believe Scripture is not just information—it is formation.

## Why We Love Scripture exists

We live in a time where there is no shortage of content—but a shortage of depth.

We Love Scripture exists to help believers slow down, return to Scripture, and rediscover the joy of God's Word.

Not as something to consume quickly—but something to dwell in deeply.
